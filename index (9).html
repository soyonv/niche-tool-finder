Slug Generator

Turn titles, phrases or a whole list of keywords into clean URL slugs.

Ready when you are.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or logged — no account, no server, no limits.

How to use it

  1. Paste the titles, phrases or keywords — one per line for bulk work.
  2. Choose the separator: hyphen (default) or underscore.
  3. Decide whether accented characters should be transliterated (café → cafe).
  4. Copy the column of slugs — order matches your input line for line.

Options explained

Separator
The character placed between words. Hyphens are the default and the style Google displays in results; underscores suit file names or Python-style identifiers.
Transliterate accented characters
Strips diacritics so “Crème Brûlée” becomes “creme-brulee”. Turn it off to keep native scripts intact (Unicode slugs are valid in URLs).

Frequently asked questions

Does one line produce one slug?

Yes — input and output stay line-aligned, so you can paste a column of titles and copy back a column of slugs.

What happens to emoji and punctuation?

They are removed and their surrounding gaps collapsed, so “🚀 Launch Day!” becomes “launch-day”.

Are my keywords uploaded?

No. Slug generation is pure client-side JavaScript — nothing is sent to a server or stored.

Why are my Chinese/Arabic titles not ASCII?

By default the tool keeps Unicode letters rather than destroying them — Unicode slugs work in modern browsers. For strict ASCII, run the output through Find & Replace to strip non-Latin characters.

Is the output unique?

Slugs are not deduplicated automatically — pair this tool with Remove Duplicate Lines if you need unique slugs.
