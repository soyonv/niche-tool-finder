Extract Emails, URLs & Numbers

Pull every email address, link or number out of messy text.

one item per line
Options

Ready when you are.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or logged — no account, no server, no limits.

How to use it

  1. Paste the messy text — emails, links or numbers inside it do not need to be separated.
  2. Choose what to extract: emails, URLs, numbers, or all three at once.
  3. Leave deduplicate on to collapse repeats; add sorting if you want an ordered list.
  4. Copy the extracted column(s).

Options explained

Extract
Pick one target or “All three”. With “All three”, results are grouped: emails first, then URLs, then numbers, each list on its own lines.
Deduplicate
Removes repeats so each unique value appears exactly once (on by default).
Sort results A→Z
Alphabetises the extracted values; numbers are ordered numerically when the numeric list is extracted on its own.

Worked example

Input

Hi team — ping ada@example.com or grace@example.com.
Docs: https://example.com/docs and https://example.com/api
Budget is 1200.50 and 87 invoices went out.

Output

ada@example.com
grace@example.com

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle links wrapped in brackets or trailing commas?

Yes — trailing punctuation such as periods, commas and closing brackets is trimmed from extracted URLs.

Will it find emails written as images or with [at]?

No — only literal email addresses in standard form. Obfuscated addresses would first need Find & Replace to restore the “@”.

Are numbers matched with decimals and signs?

Yes: 1,234.5 and -42 both match, including comma- or dot-decimal formats.

Is the text I paste uploaded?

No. Extraction runs locally in the browser; nothing is transmitted or stored.
