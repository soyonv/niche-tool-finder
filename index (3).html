Remove Extra Spaces

Trim stray spaces, collapse double spaces and clean tabs.

one item per line
Options

Ready when you are.

Runs in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded, stored or logged — no account, no server, no limits.

How to use it

  1. Paste the text that needs a cleanup pass.
  2. Pick which passes to run: collapse repeated spaces, trim line edges, convert tabs to spaces.
  3. Check the “characters removed” counter to confirm the change is what you expect.
  4. Copy or download the tidy result.

Options explained

Collapse repeated spaces
Turns runs of two or more spaces into a single space anywhere in the line.
Trim start and end of each line
Removes leading and trailing spaces and tabs from every line — the most common cause of failed spreadsheet matches.
Replace tabs with one space
Flattens tab characters into a single space. Keep it off when you are cleaning code or tab-separated data.

Frequently asked questions

Will it destroy my indentation?

Only if you enable trimming — that removes leading whitespace on purpose. With trim off, collapse still fixes double spaces inside the line while indentation survives.

Does it remove non-breaking spaces?

Not here — non-breaking spaces are invisible Unicode characters, handled specifically by the Remove Invisible Characters tool, which also reports exactly what it removed.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Everything is computed in your browser; no data is sent anywhere.

Can it join wrapped lines back together?

Not directly — use Find & Replace with regex mode to replace newline patterns, or the Join Lines tool planned for the next wave.
